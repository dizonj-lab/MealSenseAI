# MealSenseAI demo video

A five-minute narrated tour of MealSenseAI. It opens on the problem (most meals have no label, so
people living with diabetes guess) and why it matters now, then follows Wei Ming, a fictional user
with type 2 diabetes, through a week of hawker meals and one lunch captured on camera.

| Step | Where | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Hear the voices | Buddy | `cd ~/MealSenseAI && git pull && ./demo/run.sh samples` |
| Make the video | Buddy | `./demo/run.sh` (voice af_sarah; `VOICE=…` for another) |
| Copy it | Mac | `scp 'admin@<buddy>:~/mealsense-demo-*/MealSenseAI-demo.mp4' ~/Downloads/` |

Result: `~/mealsense-demo-<time>/MealSenseAI-demo.mp4` (1080p, narration, music, subtitle track),
`.srt`, and `PHOTO-CREDITS.md` for the video's description.

The voice is Kokoro-82M, an open neural text-to-speech model run locally in a container (the same
image as CEREBRO's demo; it is reused if present). The music is generated by `music.py`: 124 BPM,
four-on-the-floor, brighter and busier than CEREBRO's, and it dips under the voice. Scenes are timed
to their lines, and any stretch where the screen only waits is sped up, so the video plays without
pauses.

## What runs where

- `run.sh` starts the Compose project **mealsense-demo** (`docker-compose.demo.yml`) from the live
  app's images, with its own database and photo volume, on `127.0.0.1:8380`. The live MealSenseAI
  and its data are not touched. `./demo/run.sh down` stops it and keeps its data.
- `photos.py` runs in the backend image. It searches Wikimedia Commons for Singapore hawker dishes,
  keeps only CC0, public-domain, CC BY and CC BY-SA files (never NC or ND), and keeps, for each
  dish, the photo MealSenseAI's own model recognises best. It writes `photos/` (git-ignored) with
  `CREDITS.md`. To use your own photo instead, put it at `photos/<dish>.jpg` with its credit.
- `seed.py` creates `weiming@mealsense.example` with a type 2 diabetes profile and 13 meals over
  the past week, each captured and analysed through the API like a real capture.
- `record.mjs` drives a browser through 13 scenes (`narration.json`): title cards for the problem,
  then the landing page, profile, dashboard, a chicken rice lunch captured and reviewed, its
  glucose and energy meaning, the portion simulator, a walk what-if, the Meal Advisor and
  Nutrition Insights. It writes the silent video and `scenes.json`.
- `voice.py speak` makes the narration first; `record.mjs` times each scene to its line;
  `voice.py build` tightens idle stretches, lays the lines back to back, adds the music with
  ducking and the subtitles.

## Changing it

- Words: edit `narration.json`. Spoken-only spellings (MealSenseAI, Nutri-Grade, nasi lemak) are
  in its `pronounce` map; the subtitles keep the written form.
- Actions: edit the matching `scene(...)` in `record.mjs`.
- Dark theme: `THEME=dark ./demo/run.sh record`.
- The Meal Advisor uses its rule engine in the demo so every take reads the same. To have a local
  model word its answers: `DEMO_OLLAMA_URL=http://host.docker.internal:11434 ./demo/run.sh`.

The figures on the "Why it matters now" card are the ones the project proposal cites: WHO (about
830 million people with diabetes, 2022), MOH (about 8.5% of Singapore adults, 2023, and the
projection of up to a million residents by 2050 without effective prevention), and the Nutri-Grade
before-and-after study (about 18% less household sugar from pre-packed drinks). The nutrition label
on the first card is illustrative. Everything in the app is real output from the running model and
rules on the fictional account; Wei Ming and his meals are invented.
